# Northwind Fitness Studio — FAQs
Edit this file with your own questions and answers. Each "## " heading is one question.

## What are your opening hours?
We're open Monday to Friday 6 am to 10 pm, and Saturday and Sunday 7 am to 8 pm. We're closed on national holidays.

## Where are you located?
We're at 12 Park Street, opposite the city library. There's free parking behind the building for members.

## How much does membership cost?
Monthly membership is 2,500 rupees, or 25,000 rupees a year (two months free). Students get 20% off with a valid ID.

## Do you offer a free trial?
Yes. Your first class is free. Book it here or reply with your name and a preferred day, and our team will confirm.

## How do I book a class?
Book any class in our app or reply with the class name and time. Classes open for booking 7 days ahead.

## What classes do you run?
Strength, HIIT, yoga, spin and mobility. Beginners are welcome in every class; coaches show easier options.

## Can I cancel or pause my membership?
You can pause for up to 2 months a year or cancel with 30 days' notice. Tell us here and a team member will help.

## Do you have personal trainers?
Yes. Personal training sessions are 1,200 rupees each or 10,000 rupees for 10. Every new member gets a free 20-minute starter session.

## Is there a locker room and shower?
Yes, with free lockers (bring your own lock), showers, towels and a hair dryer.

## Can I bring a friend?
Members can bring a guest once a month for free. Guests sign a short waiver at the front desk.
