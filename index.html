WhatsApp AI Front Desk

An open-source assistant that answers your customers on WhatsApp and your website — from your own FAQs, 24×7 — and hands the chat to a person when it should.

Your FAQs

Paste your own questions and answers (each ## Question followed by its answer), then ask the bot on the right. Everything runs in your browser.

This demo runs in FAQ-only mode. Deploy the server with an AI key to get natural, grounded answers and a WhatsApp number.

AI
Front deskvirtual assistant · replies instantly
1. Write your FAQsOne Markdown file. Each heading is a question; the text under it is the answer.
2. Connect WhatsAppPoint the WhatsApp Cloud API webhook at /webhook. Add an Anthropic key for natural answers, or run FAQ-only.
3. People take overAsks for a person, complaints, refunds or unknown questions alert your team and pause the bot in that chat.
